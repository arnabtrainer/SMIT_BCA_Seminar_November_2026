### Outcomes available in Google NotebookLM against shared resources

- Audio Overview (AI Podcast) – Converts resources into an AI-generated audio discussion or explanation.
- Video Overview – Creates a narrated visual explanation of the source material.
- Mind Maps – Visualizes topics, subtopics, and relationships between concepts.
- Slide Decks – Generates presentation slides based on the uploaded resources.
- Infographics – Presents important information, processes, and comparisons visually.
- Reports – Generates structured reports, summaries, and briefing documents.
- Study Guides – Creates organized learning and revision materials.
- Flashcards – Generates question-and-answer cards for revision.
- Quizzes – Creates practice questions to assess learners' understanding.
- Source Summaries – Summarizes individual documents or multiple resources.
- Source-Grounded Q&A – Answers questions using shared resources, with citations to supporting passages.
- FAQs – Extracts frequently asked questions and prepares answers.
- MCQ Question Banks – Generates multiple-choice questions with options and answer keys.
- Lesson Plans – Develops session-wise teaching plans and learning objectives.
- Trainer Guides – Prepares trainer notes and instructional guidance.
- Learner Handouts – Creates structured learning notes for participants.
- Practical Assignments – Develops exercises and assignments based on the source material.
- Comparative Analysis – Compares documents, policies, manuals, or reports.
- Gap Analysis – Identifies missing topics, content gaps, and areas needing improvement.
- Key Concept Extraction – Extracts definitions, formulas, principles, and important facts.
- Research Synthesis – Combines findings across multiple sources into a consolidated overview.
- Process Documentation – Organizes procedures, workflows, and step-by-step instructions.
- Executive Briefings – Condenses extensive documents into concise management summaries.
- Revision Notes – Creates concise notes for examinations, workshops, and corporate training.
- Glossaries – Compiles key terms and their definitions from the shared resources.

Note: Availability of dedicated Studio outputs and export options may vary by NotebookLM version and subscription plan. Some outcomes, such as trainer guides, assignments, and comparative analyses, are produced through custom prompts in NotebookLM Chat rather than as dedicated Studio tools.

# 🤖 AI in Action: Learn • Create • Build

### Exploring Artificial Intelligence Through Real-World Applications

**A practical, interactive, and reusable AI learning programme for students, educators, professionals, and curious beginners.**

---

## 🌟 About This Programme

**AI in Action: Learn • Create • Build** introduces Artificial Intelligence through relatable situations and practical projects. Rather than concentrating on technical definitions or a long catalogue of software tools, the programme shows how AI can help people **understand information, produce creative content, develop working applications, and make informed decisions**.

The learning journey progresses naturally from **discovering AI**, to **learning with AI**, **creating with AI**, **building with AI**, and finally **reflecting on the experience** through a knowledge challenge and participant feedback.

The programme is designed to remain **independent of any specific college, course, company, or industry**. Its examples and practical exercises can be adapted to academic seminars, faculty-development programmes, professional workshops, corporate learning events, and self-directed study.

### 🎯 Overall Purpose

- **Demystify AI:** Explain AI, Machine Learning, and Generative AI in everyday language.
- **Demonstrate practical value:** Show real ways to improve learning, communication, creativity, and productivity.
- **Encourage hands-on exploration:** Let participants see how useful outputs emerge from clear instructions and suitable source materials.
- **Develop AI literacy:** Introduce prompt design, source verification, iteration, testing, privacy, and responsible use.
- **Inspire continued learning:** Help beginners feel confident enough to explore AI independently after the programme.

> **Programme message:** AI is most useful when human curiosity, creativity, judgement, and verification work together with the technology.

---

## 👥 Target Audience

No prior AI knowledge or programming experience is required for the core activities.

| Audience | Why the programme is useful |
|---|---|
| 🎓 **Prospective and newly admitted students** | Discover how AI can support learning and introduce future possibilities. |
| 💻 **Undergraduate students** (BCA, B.Sc., B.Tech, and related programmes) | Explore study assistance, media creation, and beginner-friendly software prototyping. |
| 🧑‍💻 **Postgraduate students** (MCA, M.Tech, MBA, and other disciplines) | Apply AI to research materials, presentations, projects, and productivity. |
| 🧑‍🏫 **Faculty, trainers, and researchers** | Transform teaching resources, design learning activities, and explore source-based analysis. |
| 🧑‍💼 **Corporate employees and working professionals** | Explore knowledge synthesis, promotional media, task planning, and responsible workplace use. |
| 🌍 **General learners and entrepreneurs** | Understand how an idea can be transformed into an informative, creative, or functional result. |

**Teaching philosophy:** Start with familiar problems, demonstrate an achievable solution, involve participants, and explain how to verify the result.

---

## 🧭 The Five-Module Learning Journey

| Module | Title | Central question | Main experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | 🌟 **Discover the Power of AI** | *What is AI, and where do we already use it?* | Everyday AI applications, foundational concepts, and better prompting. |
| **02** | 📚 **AI Can Help Me Learn** | *Can AI turn my reference materials into useful knowledge?* | A source-based learning project using **Gemini Notebook**. |
| **03** | 🎬 **AI Can Help Me Create** | *Can I create a cinematic product advertisement using AI?* | A real-product promotional video workflow from brief to final edit. |
| **04** | 🛠️ **AI Can Help Me Build** | *Can I create a working application using natural language?* | Develop, test, and improve **My Smart Planner** using Claude. |
| **05** | 🚀 **Your AI Journey Starts Here!** | *What have I learned, and how will I use it?* | Knowledge challenge, participant feedback, and next steps. |

The modules are connected but modular: the application-building project may be presented as a full build or as a demonstration of a prepared, working application. The conclusion, quiz, and feedback remain part of the programme in either case.

---

## 🌟 Module 01 — Discover the Power of AI

**Theme:** Understanding AI and its everyday applications.

### Purpose

Create curiosity and establish a clear, nontechnical foundation for everything that follows. Participants recognise that they may already encounter AI through applications they use every day.

### Key Topics

- 🤖 **What is Artificial Intelligence?** An approachable explanation of systems that perform tasks involving prediction, recognition, language, and other capabilities.
- 🧩 **AI, Machine Learning, and Generative AI:** Understand the relationship among these concepts through simple examples.
- 📱 **AI around us:** Recommendations, navigation, translation, visual recognition, conversational assistants, and more.
- ✍️ **Prompting basics:** Why context, clear tasks, desired formats, and follow-up questions improve results.
- 🛡️ **Responsible use:** Check important information, protect sensitive data, and maintain human judgement.

### Practical Experience

Compare a vague AI request with a clear, contextual prompt based on an everyday planning or explanation task. Discuss whether the result is useful and how it can be verified.

### Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to recognise common uses of AI, distinguish AI from Generative AI at a basic level, write a clearer prompt, and explain why AI-generated information should not be accepted uncritically.

---

## 📚 Module 02 — AI Can Help Me Learn

**Theme:** Transform information into knowledge using **Gemini Notebook** (formerly NotebookLM).

### Purpose

Demonstrate how AI can work with **the learner's own reference sources**, making information easier to understand, explore, organise, discuss, and review.

### Demonstration Project — *Stay Smart in the Digital World*

A reusable **digital-safety learning notebook** brings together complementary source materials:

| Input | Role in the project |
|---|---|
| 📕 **PDF** | Digital-safety fundamentals and core concepts. |
| 📘 **DOCX** | Fictional, real-life safety scenarios and decisions. |
| 📊 **PPTX** | Visual explanations, checklists, and examples. |
| 📝 **TXT** | Glossary, quick-reference terms, and recall points. |
| 🎥 **YouTube link** | An educational video transcript, where supported. |
| 🌐 **Web link** | Published external guidance for comparison and corroboration. |

### Key Capabilities Explored

- 💬 **Source-grounded Q&A and citations:** Ask questions and examine the supporting passages.
- 📄 **Summaries and reports:** Condense source materials into useful explanations and structured content.
- 🧠 **Mind maps:** Visualise relationships among important concepts.
- 📝 **Quizzes, MCQ banks, and flashcards:** Turn source material into self-assessment resources.
- 📖 **Glossaries:** Identify and explain important terms.
- 🎧 **Audio Overviews:** Explore AI-generated spoken explanations or discussions.
- 🎬 **Video Overviews:** Turn source material into a narrated visual explanation.
- 🎨 **Infographics and slide decks:** Present important information visually for learning and communication.

Some outputs are dedicated Notebook features; others, such as custom MCQ banks and glossaries, can be developed using suitable instructions. Actual capabilities and availability depend on the account and product version.

### Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to import different sources, ask focused questions, evaluate citations, create multiple learning formats, and recognise the limitations of source-based AI responses.

**Key takeaway:** A document can become an interactive learning resource—but source checking is still essential.

---

## 🎬 Module 03 — AI Can Help Me Create

**Theme:** From a real product to a cinematic AI advertisement.

### Purpose

Show that AI-assisted creative production involves **research, storytelling, visual design, motion generation, narration, music, editing, and quality control**—not just generating an attractive picture.

### Demonstration Project — *Every Journey Begins with a Moment*

Create an original, short promotional-film concept for a **Milton Atlantis Thermosteel Water Bottle**, using product information from the [official Milton website](https://www.milton.in/).

The advertisement is an **independent educational exercise**, not an official campaign or endorsement by the manufacturer. Product specifications, branding, and any marketing claims must be checked against authorised references.

### End-to-End Creative Workflow

1. 📋 **Research the product and define the creative brief:** Product, target audience, key message, visual style, and intended outcome.
2. ✍️ **Develop the storyline and narration:** Use ChatGPT as a creative writing and ideation assistant.
3. 🧩 **Create scene-by-scene prompts:** Specify subjects, actions, framing, camera movement, lighting, colour, and visual consistency; structured JSON can serve as a production record.
4. 🖼️ **Generate storyboard images:** Create coherent frames with consistent people, product appearance, and environments.
5. 🎥 **Animate the frames:** Use Google Vids with an available video-generation model to create **actual moving footage**, including human activity and controlled camera movement.
6. 🎙️ **Produce natural AI narration:** Use an appropriate text-to-speech tool, such as MiniMax Speech, and review the spoken result.
7. 🎵 **Produce background music:** Generate or select a suitably licensed instrumental soundtrack, independently of narration.
8. 🎞️ **Assemble the advertisement:** Arrange moving clips, adjust sound, add transitions, subtitles, product title, and any authorised branding.
9. ✅ **Review and export:** Check the story, motion realism, product consistency, audio balance, permissions, and finished MP4.

### Main Tools

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) — creative concept, script, prompt development, and storyboard support.
- [Google Vids](https://workspace.google.com/products/vids/) — AI-assisted video generation and editing, where enabled.
- [MiniMax](https://www.minimax.io/) — speech and music generation, subject to feature availability and licensing.

### Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to explain an AI video-production pipeline, create more precise visual prompts, distinguish **real image-to-video motion** from a static-image slideshow, and understand why iteration and professional editing remain important.

**Key takeaway:** AI can assist filmmaking, but convincing results require sound creative direction, genuine motion, and careful selection and editing.

---

## 🛠️ Module 04 — AI Can Help Me Build

**Theme:** Turn a natural-language requirement into a working application.

### Purpose

Demonstrate how an AI assistant can help users prototype functional software, even when they do not begin by writing code manually. The experience emphasises **requirements, iteration, testing, and usability**.

### Demonstration Project — *My Smart Planner*

A responsive task-planning application designed for both **Study Mode** and **Work Mode**. It includes:

- ✅ Add, edit, delete, complete, and reopen tasks.
- 📅 Organise deadlines, task categories, and priorities.
- 📊 Display progress and completion statistics.
- 🔎 Search, filter, and sort tasks.
- 🌗 Switch between light and dark themes.
- 💾 Save data locally and support backup, restore, and export functions.
- 📱 Provide a mobile-friendly interface.

### Practical Workflow

**Describe the idea → Generate an interactive app using Claude Artifacts → Test basic actions → Refine a feature → Retest → Explore the working result.**

A prepared **offline HTML version** allows users to explore the application without a network connection. That version is an ordinary local task-management application: it was developed as an educational AI-assisted example but **does not call an AI model while it runs**.

### Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to describe app requirements clearly, test whether the generated features work, request improvements, distinguish AI-assisted *development* from AI-powered *runtime functionality*, and appreciate the need for validation and privacy-conscious design.

**Key takeaway:** AI can accelerate the move from an idea to a prototype; the creator remains responsible for checking the result.

---

## 🚀 Module 05 — Your AI Journey Starts Here!

**Theme:** Reflect, test your knowledge, share feedback, and plan what comes next.

### Purpose

Consolidate learning through a friendly knowledge challenge and invite participants to evaluate the programme. The module encourages reflection and continued exploration rather than treating AI learning as a one-time event.

### Knowledge Challenge

An **eight-question multiple-choice challenge** reviews:

1. 🤖 Everyday applications of AI.
2. ✍️ Effective prompt writing.
3. 📚 Source-grounded learning in Gemini Notebook.
4. 🔍 Verification of important AI-generated information.
5. 🎬 Image-to-video generation.
6. 🎙️ The roles of narration and background music.
7. 🛠️ Testing AI-assisted applications.
8. 🛡️ Responsible and ethical AI use.

Each question has four answer choices and an explanation. In the Markdown question bank, answers can be placed in expandable **Answer & Explanation** sections for self-study.

### Participant Feedback

A brief, reusable questionnaire gathers feedback on overall experience, practical usefulness, preferred demonstrations, confidence in using AI, presentation pace, future learning interests, and suggestions for improvement.

A single **Google Form**, when created and published, can combine graded quiz questions and ungraded feedback. A live form link or QR code is optional; the question bank can also be used without an online form.

### Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to identify the programme's major lessons, reflect on responsible AI use, and select one practical application to explore independently.

**Key takeaway:** The real value of AI begins when we apply what we have learned thoughtfully.

---

## 🧪 Learning Approach

The programme emphasises the following recurring cycle:

**Understand a problem → Give clear instructions → Generate or explore an output → Review the result → Improve it → Apply the learning.**

Across the five modules, participants encounter a mix of:

- 💡 Familiar examples and accessible explanations.
- 🖥️ Live demonstrations of practical AI workflows.
- 🧠 Questions, small challenges, and audience participation.
- 🎨 Visual storytelling and creative production.
- ✅ Output review, testing, and source verification.

The emphasis can be adapted to different backgrounds without changing the programme's overall learning objectives.

## 🛡️ Responsible AI Principles

Participants are encouraged to:

- **Verify important information:** Check factual claims and the evidence behind them.
- **Protect privacy:** Do not upload sensitive personal, educational, or organisational information without appropriate authorisation.
- **Respect copyright and consent:** Use images, voices, music, and brands only with suitable rights or permissions.
- **Avoid false impressions:** Clearly distinguish a concept advertisement or AI-generated scene from genuine footage or an officially endorsed campaign.
- **Test generated applications:** Confirm that features, calculations, data handling, and interface behaviour work as expected.
- **Keep humans accountable:** AI suggestions support decisions; they do not remove the need for informed human judgement.

---

## 🌱 Expected Programme Outcomes

After completing the programme, participants should be better able to:

1. **Recognise** useful applications of AI in everyday life, learning, and work.
2. **Communicate** with AI tools through clearer prompts and iterative instructions.
3. **Explore and verify** information using source-based knowledge tools.
4. **Design and produce** a creative AI-assisted video workflow with narration and music.
5. **Prototype and test** a practical application using AI-assisted development.
6. **Evaluate** AI outputs with attention to accuracy, privacy, authenticity, and limitations.
7. **Continue independently** with relevant tools, projects, and learning resources.
