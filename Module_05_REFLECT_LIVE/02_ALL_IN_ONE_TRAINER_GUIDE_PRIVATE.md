### 🔴 Q1. Which is a familiar example of AI in everyday life?

A. A USB drive storing a document <br>
B. Video recommendations based on viewing interests <br>
C. A printer printing a PDF <br>
D. A keyboard typing a message <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: B. Video recommendations based on viewing interests**

**Explanation:** Recommendation systems can analyse viewing behaviour and content patterns to suggest relevant videos. The other activities do not inherently require AI.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q2. Which instruction is most likely to produce a useful AI response?

A. Explain it. <br>
B. Write about computers, and make it nice. <br>
C. Explain computer memory to a beginner with an example and three key points. <br>
D. Give me everything you know. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: C. Explain computer memory to a beginner with an example and three key points.**

**Explanation:** A clear task, audience, context and expected format help an AI assistant deliver a more relevant answer. Verification is still important.

</details>

### 🔴 Q3. What makes Gemini Notebook particularly useful for source-based learning?

A. It uses supplied sources and links answers to supporting passages. <br>
B. It guarantees every uploaded document is accurate. <br>
C. It replaces the need to read original sources. <br>
D. It checks every claim against the entire internet automatically. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: A. It uses supplied sources and links answers to supporting passages.**

**Explanation:** Source-grounded Q&A lets learners trace answers to the uploaded material. Source citations support checking but do not guarantee the source itself is correct.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q4. Why should we verify important answers generated by AI?

A. AI never provides factual information. <br>
B. AI responses are always verified automatically. <br>
C. A confident tone proves a statement is correct. <br>
D. An AI response can sound convincing while containing errors. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: D. An AI response can sound convincing while containing errors.**

**Explanation:** Generative AI can produce plausible but inaccurate claims. Check important facts against trustworthy primary or authoritative sources.

</details>

### 🔴 Q5. In AI video production, what does image-to-video mean?

A. Animating a reference image into moving footage <br>
B. Exporting one still frame from a movie <br>
C. Shrinking an image file <br>
D. Converting a photo to a text document <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: A. Animating a reference image into moving footage**

**Explanation:** Image-to-video generation uses a still image plus motion instructions to produce moving scenes, often with camera, subject and environmental motion.

</details>

### 🔴 Q6. In a product advertisement, what is the main role of voiceover compared with background music?

A. Voiceover controls camera motion; music controls focus. <br>
B. They both replace the need for visual storytelling. <br>
C. Voiceover communicates spoken information; music supports the mood. <br>
D. Background music automatically verifies product claims. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: C. Voiceover communicates spoken information; music supports the mood.**

**Explanation:** Narration delivers the spoken message, while music shapes atmosphere and emotional pacing. They should be mixed so speech stays understandable.

</details>

---

### 🔴 Q7. What should you do after an AI tool generates a task-planning app?

A. Share it immediately without using it. <br>
B. Test key interactions, calculations and data handling. <br>
C. Remove input validation to make it simpler. <br>
D. Avoid requesting improvements to the design. <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: B. Test key interactions, calculations and data handling.**

**Explanation:** Generated software must be tested. Create, edit and complete tasks, check progress calculations, and verify data storage and input validation.

</details>

### 🔴 Q8. Which choice best demonstrates responsible AI use?

A. Sharing passwords to get more personalised answers <br>
B. Publishing generated claims without checking them <br>
C. Uploading confidential information to every AI tool <br>
D. Verifying outputs and protecting private or sensitive information <br>

<details>
<summary><b>Answer & Explanation</b></summary>

**Answer: D. Verifying outputs and protecting private or sensitive information**

**Explanation:** Responsible use means checking important claims, maintaining privacy, respecting permissions and applying human judgement.

</details>

## 🗣️ Participant feedback — one combined Google Form

Use **one form** containing two sections: **A. Knowledge Quiz** (eight graded MCQs) and **B. Session Feedback** (seven ungraded questions). The form must be created in the trainer's Google account. No live form, public link, or QR code is supplied in this package because those are account-specific.

| No. | Feedback question | Type | Required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | How would you rate the overall learning experience? | Scale (1–5) | Yes |
| F2 | How relevant were the examples to your studies or work? | Scale (1–5) | Yes |
| F3 | How confident do you now feel about trying AI tools yourself? | Scale (1–5) | Yes |
| F4 | How was the pace of the session? | Multiple choice: Too slow / About right / Too fast | Yes |
| F5 | Which part was most useful to you? | Multiple choice: Discover / Learn / Create / Build | Yes |
| F6 | What topic would you like to explore next? | Multiple choice: Research / AI video / App building / Automation / Other | Optional |

### 🧩 Google Forms — manual setup (beginner-friendly)

1. Open **https://forms.google.com/** (or **https://g.co/createaquiz**).
2. Create **AI in Action — Knowledge Challenge & Feedback**. Turn on **Settings → Make this a quiz**.
3. Add a quiz section with eight **Multiple choice** items. Use the MCQs above, assign **1 point** to each and enter the corresponding **Answer key**. Add concise answer explanations as answer feedback.
4. Add a new section titled **Your Voice Matters — Feedback**. Insert the seven *ungraded* questions listed above. Use scale 1–5 where specified.
5. Under response settings, **turn OFF Collect email addresses** if responses should be anonymous. Also avoid requiring names, employee IDs or registration numbers. Do not enable a sign-in requirement simply to limit one response per person.
6. Choose whether to **release grades immediately or after review**. Test both quiz and feedback before distributing.
7. **Publish/share** the form so the intended audience can access it. Copy the **responder** URL—not the editing URL. Verify in an incognito/private window with a test submission.
8. Generate a QR code from the actual published responder URL using a trusted QR tool or browser feature. Insert that QR image into Slide 12 shortly before your event, or show the link outside the deck. **Never use a fabricated QR code.**
9. At the event, invite participants to submit once. Afterwards, open **Responses → Summary** to inspect scores and aggregate feedback; optionally link responses to Google Sheets for analysis.

**Important:** With anonymous responses, you can see aggregate quiz results but cannot reliably identify individual respondents. Response access and publication controls may differ between personal and organisational accounts.

Official help: https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7032287 and https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2839588

### ⚙️ Optional automated form creation — Apps Script

The following optional script creates one quiz-and-feedback Google Form. **Do not run it in a shared corporate account without permission.** It creates a new form; it does not automatically distribute or publish it. Review the generated form and publish/share it manually.

1. Open https://script.google.com/ and choose **New project**.
2. Paste the following code, run `createAIWorkshopForm`, grant authorised access, and review the edit and responder URLs printed in the execution log.
3. Check the answer key, wording, release settings and access policy before sharing.

```javascript
function createAIWorkshopForm() {
  const form = FormApp.create('AI in Action — Knowledge Challenge & Feedback');
  form.setDescription('Learn • Create • Build | Friendly quiz and optional feedback');
  form.setIsQuiz(true);
  form.setCollectEmail(false);
  form.setConfirmationMessage('Thank you for participating and sharing your feedback!');
  form.addSectionHeaderItem().setTitle('Section A — AI Knowledge Challenge');

  const quiz = [
    {question:"Q1. Which is a familiar example of AI in everyday life?", options:["A USB drive storing a document", "Video recommendations based on viewing interests", "A printer printing a PDF", "A keyboard typing a message"], answer:1, why:"Recommendation systems can analyse viewing behaviour and content patterns to suggest relevant videos. The other activities do not inherently require AI."},
    {question:"Q2. Which instruction is most likely to produce a useful AI response?", options:["Explain it.", "Write about computers, and make it nice.", "Explain computer memory to a beginner with an example and three key points.", "Give me everything you know."], answer:2, why:"A clear task, audience, context and expected format help an AI assistant deliver a more relevant answer. Verification is still important."},
    {question:"Q3. What makes Gemini Notebook particularly useful for source-based learning?", options:["It uses supplied sources and links answers to supporting passages.", "It guarantees every uploaded document is accurate.", "It replaces the need to read original sources.", "It checks every claim against the entire internet automatically."], answer:0, why:"Source-grounded Q&A lets learners trace answers to the uploaded material. Source citations support checking but do not guarantee the source itself is correct."},
    {question:"Q4. Why should we verify important answers generated by AI?", options:["AI never provides factual information.", "AI responses are always verified automatically.", "A confident tone proves a statement is correct.", "An AI response can sound convincing while containing errors."], answer:3, why:"Generative AI can produce plausible but inaccurate claims. Check important facts against trustworthy primary or authoritative sources."},
    {question:"Q5. In AI video production, what does image-to-video mean?", options:["Animating a reference image into moving footage", "Exporting one still frame from a movie", "Shrinking an image file", "Converting a photo to a text document"], answer:0, why:"Image-to-video generation uses a still image plus motion instructions to produce moving scenes, often with camera, subject and environmental motion."},
    {question:"Q6. In a product advertisement, what is the main role of voiceover compared with background music?", options:["Voiceover controls camera motion; music controls focus.", "They both replace the need for visual storytelling.", "Voiceover communicates spoken information; music supports the mood.", "Background music automatically verifies product claims."], answer:2, why:"Narration delivers the spoken message, while music shapes atmosphere and emotional pacing. They should be mixed so speech stays understandable."},
    {question:"Q7. What should you do after an AI tool generates a task-planning app?", options:["Share it immediately without using it.", "Test key interactions, calculations and data handling.", "Remove input validation to make it simpler.", "Avoid requesting improvements to the design."], answer:1, why:"Generated software must be tested. Create, edit and complete tasks, check progress calculations, and verify data storage and input validation."},
    {question:"Q8.   ];
  quiz.forEach(q => {
    const item = form.addMultipleChoiceItem().setTitle(q.question).setRequired(true);
    item.setChoices(q.options.map((label, index) => item.createChoice(label, index === q.answer)));
    item.setPoints(1);
    const feedback = FormApp.createFeedback().setText(q.why).build();
    item.setFeedbackForCorrect(feedback);
    item.setFeedbackForIncorrect(feedback);
  });
```
