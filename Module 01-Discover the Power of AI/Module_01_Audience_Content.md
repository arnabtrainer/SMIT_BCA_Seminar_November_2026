## 🌍 AI Is Already Around Us

| Everyday experience | How AI can help | What still needs human judgement |
|---|---|---|
| ▶️ Video recommendations | Suggest content based on signals of relevance and interest | Decide whether the suggestion is worthwhile |
| 🗺️ Navigation and journey estimates | Help predict traffic conditions and travel time | Check the actual route and changing conditions |
| 🌐 Translation tools | Convert information from one language to another | Check important meanings and nuance |
| 📧 Spam detection | Recognise patterns associated with unwanted messages | Review messages that may be incorrectly classified |

These systems use AI for particular features; **not every part of a digital app is AI**.

## ⚙️ Is Every Automated Task Artificial Intelligence?

**No.** Automation means a system carries out a process without repeated manual effort. Some automation uses fixed instructions; other systems use AI.

- **Fixed-rule example:** A calculator follows arithmetic rules to multiply two numbers.
- **Machine-learning example:** A spam classifier can learn patterns from labelled examples and then classify new messages.

**Remember:** Smart-looking software is not necessarily AI. Ask *what the system actually does*.

## 🧠 AI, Machine Learning, and Generative AI

### Artificial Intelligence (AI)
The broad field of designing systems capable of tasks associated with intelligence, including prediction, recognition, reasoning, or language understanding.

### Machine Learning (ML)
An approach within AI in which models learn useful patterns from data or examples. For instance, a model can learn to classify messages as likely spam or not spam.

### Generative AI
AI that creates content in response to inputs: explanations, images, audio, video, and code. Modern generative AI commonly uses machine learning.

**A simple way to remember:**

| Concept | Think of it as… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🤖 AI | The **broader field** | A system that recognises or recommends |
| 📊 ML | **Learning patterns** from data | Detecting likely spam |
| 🎨 Generative AI | **Creating new content** | Drafting an explanation or generating a video |

**Important:** The labels are connected, not competing products. Not every AI system generates content, and generated content is not automatically accurate.

## 💬 Prompting: Ask Clearly, Get a Better Starting Point

A **prompt** is the instruction or request you give an AI system. A useful prompt often contains three elements:

1. **Context** — Who is this for? What background does the AI need?
2. **Task** — What exactly should be done?
3. **Output** — What form, length, or structure would be most useful?

**Vague prompt:** “Explain algorithms.”

**More helpful prompt:** “Explain the idea of an algorithm to a complete beginner using four ordered steps for planning a day trip, followed by a one-sentence definition. Use simple language and no code.”

### 🧳 Try This Everyday Example

Copy the following prompt into an AI assistant:

```text
Explain the idea of an algorithm to someone with no programming background.

Use a familiar example: planning a one-day trip.

Give:
1. Four sensible steps in the right order.
2. A one-sentence definition of an algorithm.
3. One question about what could go wrong if an important step is skipped.

Use simple English. Do not include code. Keep the reply under 100 words.
```

**Think about the result:** Is the order logical? Does the plan consider travel time or opening hours? Could a confident-sounding detail still be wrong?

Better instructions can improve usefulness, but **a polished response is not proof that every fact is correct**.

## 🔎 Evaluate, Don't Just Accept

Suppose an AI gives this illustrative plan:

1. Choose your destination.
2. Pack the items you need.
3. Travel to the destination.
4. Return home.

It is a reasonable outline, but **what might be missing?** Perhaps the route, travel time, budget, opening hours, weather, or accessibility needs. The best next step is to *review and refine* the answer—not simply copy it.

## 🛡️ Three Habits for Responsible AI

| Habit | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| ✅ **CHECK** | Verify important claims using reliable sources; test generated calculations and code. |
| 🔒 **PROTECT** | Do not enter passwords, confidential records, personal IDs, or other sensitive information without appropriate safeguards and permission. |
| 🧩 **PRACTISE** | Use AI to support your understanding and skill development, not to replace independent thinking. |

AI is a helpful assistant; **you remain responsible for decisions and outcomes**.
