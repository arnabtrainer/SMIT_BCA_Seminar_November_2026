# 🎬 Module 3 — Compact From-Scratch Video Demonstration Kit

## Your one-page orientation (PRIVATE)

**One example:** a fictional corporate client wants a short promotional film for **SmartLife**, a personal planning concept.

**Your first move:** Open `LIVE_DEMO/Module_03_Trainer_Presentation_WITH_NOTES.pptx` in Presenter View and keep `LIVE_DEMO/LIVE_DEMO_CONTROL_CARD_PRIVATE.md` beside it. Those are the only two documents you need to *read* during the live seminar.

**Your three browser tabs:**

1. https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow — create images and animate to video.
2. https://aistudio.google.com/ — generate one AI narration voice.
3. https://www.canva.com/video-editor/ — add clips, voice and music, export MP4.

**Delivery story:** Brief → two AI still images → animate both images → AI voice → licensed instrumental → Canva timeline → finished MP4.

## The honest distinction

The bundled video is a **locally assembled illustrative 20-second promotional preview**, produced using AI-generated still images, simple static-image sequencing, a basic offline synthetic voice and an original procedurally composed instrumental soundtrack. It is **not** generated in Google Flow, and the narration is **not** produced by Google AI Studio. It is there to show pacing and the intended final format if live generation is slow.

To create the actual final Flow + AI Studio + Canva movie, follow the private control sheet with your own account. The source kit intentionally contains no pre-generated Flow clips; your classroom workflow begins from text prompts.

## Presentation safeguards

The fictional project is audience-neutral and reusable for students, educators, and professional audiences. No time allocations or private speaking instructions are included in the audience PPTX or the audience Markdown. The trainer PPTX is not intended for distribution.

**Future client adaptation:** Replace the fictional product name, problem, target audience, two-image prompts, voiceover and call-to-action. Keep the workflow the same.
