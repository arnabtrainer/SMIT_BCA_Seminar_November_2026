# 🎬 Module 3 — AI Can Help Me Create

## From Idea to Finished Video: AI-Powered Promotional Filmmaking

**Training series:** AI in Action — Learn • Create • Build  
**Demonstration project:** *SmartLife — A Simpler Way to Plan Your Day*  
**Project status:** Fictional product used to explain an end-to-end workflow.

## 🌟 What can we create with AI?

An idea can become a short promotional video without starting with filmed footage, existing photographs, a recorded voice, or music. AI assists with the images, motion and narration; a video editor brings all the pieces together.

**Our creative workflow:** 📋 Client brief → ✍️ Story → 🖼️ Images → 🎥 Video clips → 🎙️ Voiceover → 🎼 Music → 🎞️ Final MP4

## 📋 1. The client brief

A fictional organisation is introducing **SmartLife**, a simple personal planning concept. It wants a friendly, optimistic promotional video for people who juggle everyday tasks.

| Creative element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Audience | Students and working professionals |
| Main problem | Busy schedules and competing priorities |
| Proposed benefit | A clearer overview of tasks and progress |
| Visual style | Cinematic, natural, warm, contemporary |
| Brand line | **SmartLife — Plan clearer. Live brighter.** |
| Video format | 16:9 landscape MP4 |

The product name and depicted interface are fictional. The video is a concept demonstration, not an advertisement for a functioning service.

## 🎞️ 2. Turn the brief into a simple story

| Scene | Story | Visual concept | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — The Challenge | A person feels overwhelmed by tasks. | A cluttered desk and an anxious expression. | Too many tasks? |
| 2 — A Better Way | A simple digital planner helps organise priorities. | The same person appears more confident while using a planning dashboard. | Plan with clarity. |
| 3 — The Brand | A clean closing card reinforces the message. | SmartLife logo-style typography and a bright slogan. | Plan clearer. Live brighter. |

A small, complete story is better than many disconnected cinematic shots.

## ✍️ 3. Write an effective AI image prompt

A useful visual prompt identifies **who or what appears, where the scene takes place, what is happening, how it should look, and what should be avoided**.

**Example — First scene image:**

```text
Create a cinematic, photorealistic, 16:9 advertising image for a fictional
productivity product called SmartLife. A young adult in a teal shirt is at
a modern desk, looking overwhelmed by everyday tasks. Show a laptop,
planner, sticky notes and a warm late-afternoon window. Soft cinematic
lighting, natural expression, credible setting, consistent character
appearance. No readable text, logos, watermarks or distorted hands.
```

**Second scene:** Reuse the first scene's character as a visual reference. Show the same person calmly using a clean planning dashboard; preserve the outfit, setting and lighting.

## 🎥 4. Bring the still images to life

In **[Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow)**, create images from the descriptions and then animate each approved image. A video-generation prompt should identify the motion, camera movement, atmosphere and any sounds.

**Example — Motion prompt:**

```text
Animate the supplied image into a realistic cinematic video.
The person glances at the laptop, takes a brief thoughtful breath,
and gently touches the planner. Add a slow camera push-in and
subtle natural movement. Preserve identity, background and outfit.
Use subtle room ambience. No dialogue, subtitles or new objects.
```

The animation may not preserve every detail perfectly; review and regenerate if needed.

## 🎙️ 5. Create narration with AI

Text-to-speech tools such as **[Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/)** can generate a voice from written narration.

**Narration script:**

> “Too many tasks, too little time? Meet SmartLife, your personal planning companion. Organise priorities, track progress, and make room for what matters. SmartLife. Plan clearer. Live brighter.”

Choose a clear, friendly voice. Download the generated audio and listen for correct pronunciation, natural pacing and consistent volume. Revise the text if necessary.

## 🎼 6. Add background music

In **[Canva Video Editor](https://www.canva.com/video-editor/)**, select a suitable free, appropriately licensed instrumental track or upload audio you are permitted to use. Keep the soundtrack quieter than the spoken narration, and add a gentle fade at the end.

## 🎞️ 7. Assemble the finished video

In Canva, place the two approved moving clips in sequence, add the closing title, upload the narration, select the background music, and align everything on the timeline. Include readable captions or titles, then export the finished video as an MP4.

Check the exported file for synchronisation, audio levels, spelling, visual continuity and correct playback.

## 🧠 8. Beyond this example

The same workflow can support an educational explainer, service introduction, product teaser, non-profit awareness message, campus campaign or internal corporate communication. Longer films can be made by connecting more short scenes, subject to editing quality and consistency.

## 🛡️ Responsible AI creativity

Use original or authorised images, scripts and music. Get consent before using identifiable real people or imitating their voices. Avoid invented product claims, fabricated endorsements and misleading depictions. Label AI-generated material when appropriate and verify rights before publication.
