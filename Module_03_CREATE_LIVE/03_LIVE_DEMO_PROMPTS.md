# 🎬 AI Can Help Me Create — Two Live Prompts

**Creative project:** One Idea Can Change Tomorrow  
**Tools:** [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow) + [Canva Video Editor](https://www.canva.com/video-editor/)  
**Theme:** Curiosity → Imagination → Creation → Impact

## ✨ 1. Text to Video — Curiosity

Copy into Flow's text-to-video workflow:

```text
Create a realistic cinematic video clip in 16:9 landscape. A young South Asian adult with wavy dark hair and glasses sits at a warm, contemporary study desk with a notebook and laptop. The person looks at scattered notes, pauses to think, then begins sketching an idea to organise everyday learning and tasks. Soft golden-hour sunlight, tasteful cyan shadows, plants and natural furnishings. One slow camera push-in, subtle believable movement, realistic face and hands, shallow depth of field. Reflective but hopeful mood. Ambient room sound only. No dialogue, readable words, logos, subtitles, watermarks or on-screen titles.
```

**Possible improvement:** “Keep the camera stationary and the hands mostly still, with one natural pen movement.”

## 🖼️ 2. Image to Video — Animate the Reference

Use **`04_Reference_Image.jpg`** as the starting frame/reference in a supported Flow video workflow. Then paste:

```text
Animate this supplied reference image into a short realistic cinematic shot. Preserve the original character, outfit, room, lighting, framing and desk objects. The person looks from the notebook to the laptop, takes a thoughtful breath and gently moves a pen to sketch an idea. Use one very slow push-in and subtle natural movements. Keep hands, face, objects and background stable. No additional people, dramatic camera cuts, readable words, captions, logos or invented interface text.
```

**Possible improvement:** “Reduce camera motion, avoid hand movement and preserve the facial features precisely.”

## 🎞️ Final film assembly

Arrange the four approved video clips in this order: **Curiosity → Imagination → Creation → Impact**. Add the titles *It Starts with a Question*, *Imagine What Is Possible*, *Turn Ideas into Action*, and *Create a Better Tomorrow*. Finish with **ONE IDEA CAN CHANGE TOMORROW**.

Use Canva to trim clips, add a separately recorded voiceover or licensed audio, and export MP4. The reference image and silent animatic in this kit are **concept illustrations**, not evidence of completed Flow generation.

**Creative checklist:** realistic motion • consistent people and setting • no accidental text • reliable rights/consent • captions and music checked before sharing.
