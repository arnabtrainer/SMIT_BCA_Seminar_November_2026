### Prompt A — Storyline and narration (ChatGPT)
```text
Act as a world-class creative director and cinematic storyteller. Create an original 20-second advertisement CONCEPT for Milton Atlantis Thermosteel Water Bottle (black, 750 ml) based only on facts on its official product page: https://www.milton.in/products/atlantis-thermosteel-water-bottle?variant=42401855340644.

Use one adult woman, one bottle, three shots (8 + 8 + 4 seconds), a morning preparation scene, a natural outdoor journey, and a refined product close. Strong, realistic human actions; cinematic lens and camera motion; warm premium colour grade. No people talking on camera; off-screen Indian-English narration only. No health claims, invented specifications, customer testimonials or brand approval claims.

Return: one-sentence idea, a concise narration that fits naturally in 18–19 seconds, an 8+8+4 storyboard table, and a short continuity description. Brand film is an unofficial educational concept.
```

### Prompt B — Detailed scene JSONs (ChatGPT)
```text
Using the approved brief above, write THREE independent structured JSON scene specifications (S01, S02, S03), one per scene, suitable for Google Vids/Veo image-to-video. Include the SAME explicit actor DNA, bottle geometry, light/colour grade and prohibited changes in EVERY scene—not only in a separate global prompt. Each scene must include: exact visual frame description, one primary physical action, shot/lens, real physical camera motion, scene ambience, narration segment, continuity DNA and negative constraints. Design real motion by actors or objects, not a digital zoom over a static photograph. Duration in final edit: S01 8 seconds, S02 8 seconds, S03 4 seconds (trimmed from a generated 8-second clip if necessary). Do not embed captions or logos in generated footage. Output clearly labelled JSON blocks, one per scene.
```


## 🎞️ ASSEMBLY — GOOGLE VIDS EDITOR

2. Put narration on a separate track; ensure its final words fit before the end card. Keep 1–2 seconds for the brand reveal.

## ✅ FIVE QUALITY CHECKS BEFORE PRESENTING THE RESULT
- **Real video motion:** walking, picking up the bottle, camera movement; not a Ken Burns slideshow.
- **Continuity:** same woman, hair, jacket, backpack and bottle geometry across scenes; no phantom hands or shape changes.
- **Natural voice:** emotionally appropriate, correct pronunciation and no robotic artifacts; narration clearly above music.
- **Accurate branding:** only verified specs and authorised product assets; no fake logos or endorsement claims.
- **Playback:** actual working MP4 with audio, fitting the storyboard; avoid declaring completion before playing and reviewing it.
