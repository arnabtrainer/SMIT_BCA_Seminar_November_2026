# Module 3 — AI Can Help Me Create

**Primary tool:** Google Flow (or another tested video-generation service available to the trainer)  
**Seminar:** AI with Applications — Learn, Create & Build Your Future  
**Target time:** 35 minutes  
**Priority:** Essential

## Purpose
Demonstrate turning a written idea into a short AI-generated video through planning, prompting, iteration, and editing. Focus on creativity rather than video-production terminology.

## One shared project
**“My BCA, My Future”** — a motivational 25–40-second fictional student journey: curiosity → classroom learning → teamwork → future possibilities. Use conceptual scenes, not fabricated footage of SMIT facilities.

## Learning outcomes
- Understand that a video begins with a clear idea and a short storyboard.
- Write a scene prompt with subject, action, setting, mood and camera direction.
- Generate or review a short video clip and identify an improvement.
- Understand how several short clips and titles become one finished film.
- Recognise ethical rules for AI video, identity and institutional representation.

## Proposed live flow (35 minutes)
1. **0–5 min — Hook:** Play a pre-generated 25–40-second final video and ask how it was created.
2. **5–11 min — Plan:** Show a simple 4-scene storyboard; let students suggest a scene or mood.
3. **11–19 min — Prompt:** Build a scene prompt aloud from audience suggestions; start one live clip generation.
4. **19–24 min — Review:** Compare a previously generated clip with its prompt. Discuss errors such as inconsistent characters or unreadable text.
5. **24–31 min — Edit:** Show a prepared project containing the clips; demonstrate adding a title, simple transitions and narration/music if licensing permits.
6. **31–35 min — Showcase and recap:** Play the finished export and ask students to suggest a new practical video application.

## Preparation
- Four already-generated clips, a short completed MP4 and a saved editable timeline.
- Tested prompts, backup screenshots, local copies of all assets and a fallback playback path.
- Confirm account credits, model availability, output rights and internet before the event.

## Alternative SMIT promotional concept
A video titled **“SMIT — Learn Today, Create Tomorrow”** can be prepared with **authorised** college images and approved branding. Label any AI-generated scenes as illustrative; never invent college facilities, placement rates or testimonials. Use the institute's formal approval before presenting the video as official advertising.

## Shortening / extension
- **Short version (25–30 min):** Use pre-generated clips; edit and explain one prompt instead of waiting for live rendering.
- **Extended version (40 min):** Generate a second prompt variation and compare outputs.

## Trainer reminder
Video rendering may take longer than anticipated. The educational takeaway is the creative workflow, not whether a live clip completes on schedule.
