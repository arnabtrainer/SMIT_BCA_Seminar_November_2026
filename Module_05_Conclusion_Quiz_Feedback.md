# Module 5 — Your AI Journey Begins Here!

**Seminar:** AI with Applications — Learn, Create & Build Your Future  
**Target time:** 20 minutes  
**Priority:** Essential — do not omit quiz or feedback

## Purpose
Reinforce three memorable ideas—AI can help me **learn**, **create**, and **build**—and collect direct evidence of student understanding and engagement. Make the questions suitable for prospective students as well as BCA seniors.

## Proposed flow (20 minutes)
1. **0–3 min — Recap:** Ask the audience to name one application for each of Learn, Create and Build.
2. **3–11 min — Live quiz:** Use 6 short multiple-choice questions with a show-of-hands poll or a QR-code form. Discuss the answers briefly.
3. **11–16 min — Student feedback:** Collect anonymous responses to a short form, with a paper or hands-up fallback.
4. **16–19 min — Open questions:** Take two or three questions from the audience.
5. **19–20 min — Closing message:** Encourage exploration, checking facts, and responsible creativity.

## Draft end-of-seminar quiz (6 MCQs)

**Q1. Which task best matches Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)?**  
A. Repairing a broken laptop  
B. Asking questions about an uploaded study document  
C. Charging a smartphone  
D. Installing an operating system  
**Answer: B.** It can help explore and understand source material.

**Q2. What makes a prompt more useful?**  
A. Making it as vague as possible  
B. Adding clear context and the desired output  
C. Using only capital letters  
D. Avoiding any examples  
**Answer: B.** Clear instructions usually guide the output better.

**Q3. What should we do when AI gives an important factual answer?**  
A. Believe it automatically  
B. Share it immediately  
C. Verify it using reliable evidence  
D. Ignore the question  
**Answer: C.** AI can make mistakes.

**Q4. What is a storyboard for an AI-generated video?**  
A. A plan of the scenes in the video  
B. A laptop repair guide  
C. A type of computer memory  
D. A final examination paper  
**Answer: A.** It helps organise the video before generation.

**Q5. When AI builds a study-planner app, what should we do next?**  
A. Assume it works perfectly  
B. Test inputs and check its behaviour  
C. Delete the app  
D. Share personal passwords  
**Answer: B.** Generated software must be tested.

**Q6. Which statement best describes AI in learning?**  
A. It makes understanding unnecessary  
B. It is always correct  
C. It can assist learning, but students still need to think critically  
D. It can replace every teacher  
**Answer: C.** AI works best as an aid to human understanding.

## Draft student feedback form
Use an anonymous form; do not require phone numbers, personal email addresses, or names.

1. **Your current stage:** Exploring admission / Newly admitted BCA / BCA 2nd or 3rd year / Other or prefer not to say.
2. **Overall usefulness:** Rate the seminar from 1 (not useful) to 5 (extremely useful).
3. **Clarity for beginners:** Rate the explanations from 1 (difficult) to 5 (very easy to follow).
4. **Most interesting demonstration:** Learning companion / AI video / Smart Study Planner / Introduction / Other.
5. **Confidence:** After the seminar, how confident are you about trying an AI tool yourself? 1–5.
6. **Next topic:** Which would you like to learn more about? AI for studies / AI video and design / Building apps / AI careers / Other.
7. **One thing you learned today:** Short optional text response.
8. **One suggestion for improvement:** Short optional text response.

## Preparation
- A six-question slide deck or quiz form.
- A separate short feedback form and one QR code linking to it.
- Printed or verbal alternatives if students do not have mobile data.
- A final slide with the three takeaway messages and tool URLs.

## Shortening / extension
- **Short version (15 min):** Use 4 MCQs, 3 core feedback questions and one final audience question.
- **Extended version (25 min):** Discuss all quiz answers and invite more questions.

## Trainer reminder
Even if the application-building demonstration overruns, preserve time for an actual quiz and student feedback. Avoid asking only technical questions; the audience may not yet have begun the BCA curriculum.
