## 🎬 YouTube video — CISA: Recognize and Report Phishing

- **Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JlQovysQBn0
- **Publisher:** Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)
- **Published:** September 28, 2023
- **Purpose:** A short public-awareness explanation of phishing; useful for asking questions based on a video transcript and contrasting it with case studies in the DOCX.
- **Import method:** In Notebook → Add source → YouTube → paste the URL.
- **Important:** Google imports the **text transcript**, not the video imagery. A public video must have usable captions. **Check import in your own Notebook account before the live session**; online availability does not guarantee a successful NotebookLM import.
- **Alternative candidate:** [CYBER.ORG × CISA — Phishing: Cyber Safety Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=08oJtFFH6a0). Captions/import must also be confirmed in advance.

## 🌍 Website — FTC: How To Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams

- **Article:** https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-avoid-phishing-scams
- **Publisher:** United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC)
- **Purpose:** Published consumer-protection guidance on identifying phishing, protecting accounts and responding to suspicious messages. Useful for grounding and verifying claims from fictional scenarios.
- **Import method:** Notebook → Add source → Website → paste the article URL.
- **Important:** Notebook generally extracts **readable webpage text**, not embedded multimedia. Check the imported source and citations.

## ✅ Classroom-use safeguards

- Never upload real passwords, OTPs, financial identifiers, student records, or confidential workplace files.
- All DOCX case studies are explicitly fictional; the `*.example` domains are **non-working illustrations**.
- Imported web/video content may change or become unavailable. Source snapshots and output generation should be tested shortly before presenting.
- Verify citations against the source passages. Notebook-generated answers, summaries, and media can still contain mistakes.
