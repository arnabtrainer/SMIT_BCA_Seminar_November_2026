## 🌟 Intended learning outputs

Summaries · Source-grounded Q&A · Mind Maps · Quizzes · Flashcards · MCQ Banks · Glossaries · Audio Overviews · Video Overviews · Infographics · Slide Decks · Reports.

**Important:** These are *target outputs*, not already-generated files. Generation must be completed in NotebookLM; some outputs are prompt-based, and Studio features may vary with account access.

## 🛡️ Safety and provenance

- This is original educational material based on general public digital-safety guidance, not copied government source documents.
- Every example case is fictional. `*.example` domains do not lead to real services.
- Do not upload private participant data, passwords, OTPs or confidential organisational information.
- Verify claims and AI-generated citations using the actual source documents.
