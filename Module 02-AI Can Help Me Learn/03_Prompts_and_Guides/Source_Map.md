# 🗂️ Source Map and Learning Objectives

**Project:** Stay Smart in the Digital World  
**Universal module:** AI Can Help Me Learn — Transform Information into Knowledge  
**Platform:** Google Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)

## 📂 Resource Types Supported by Gemini Notebook

Gemini Notebook can combine information from documents, online sources, images, audio recordings, and Google Workspace materials in a single source-based notebook.

| Resource category | Supported types / examples | Important considerations |
|---|---|---|
| 📕 Documents | PDF (`.pdf`), Microsoft Word (`.docx`), PowerPoint (`.pptx`) | Prefer clear, readable documents with properly structured content. |
| 📝 Text and data files | Plain text (`.txt`), Markdown (`.md`), CSV (`.csv`) | Useful for notes, glossaries, structured facts, and tabular data. |
| 🖼️ Images | JPG/JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, TIFF, BMP, HEIC, AVIF and other supported image formats | Image interpretation may vary with image quality and content. |
| 🎧 Audio recordings | MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC and other supported audio formats | Spoken content is transcribed on import; speech-free audio is not supported. |
| 🌐 Webpages | Public web URLs (HTML pages) | Imports accessible webpage text, not embedded videos, images, or linked subpages; paywalled pages are unsupported. |
| 🎬 YouTube videos | Public YouTube URLs with available captions | Imports the video **transcript**, not the visual footage; caption availability and language support matter. |
| 📄 Google Workspace | Google Docs, Google Slides, Google Sheets | Import supported Drive sources; Google Slides are limited to 100 slides and Google Sheets to 100,000 tokens per source. |
| 📚 Digital books | EPUB (`.epub`) and eligible Google Play Books | Eligible Play Books are imported as text. |
| 📋 Direct text | Copy-and-paste text | Useful for short notes, instructions, or original reference excerpts. |
| 💬 Gemini conversations | Supported Gemini chats added as notebook context | Availability depends on account and integration support. |

**Source limits:** Google currently documents up to **500,000 words per source**, up to **200 MB per uploaded file**, and **50 sources per notebook on the Free plan**. Limits and supported features may vary by plan and change over time.

**Important distinction:** Uploading a video file in a supported audio/transcription format does not mean Gemini Notebook analyses its visual scenes; source extraction focuses on speech/transcripts. Likewise, a YouTube link contributes its caption text, not video frames.

**Official reference:** [Google Gemini Notebook Help — Add or discover new sources](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16215270?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en)

## 📚 Resources Selected for This Project

The following six sources provide complementary perspectives on the common topic **digital safety**, without unnecessarily duplicating one another.

| Source | Type | Unique contribution | Best Notebook uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital Safety Fundamentals | PDF | Structured concepts, safe procedures, and foundation questions (sections F1–F6) | Summaries, source-grounded Q&A, mind maps, citations, Audio Overview |
| Real-World Digital Safety Cases | DOCX | Six fictional practical scenarios (C01–C06), including an ambiguous notification | Scenario MCQs, reports, case comparisons |
| Digital Safety Awareness | PPTX | Visual explanations, diagrams, checklists, and cases | Slide-deck synthesis, infographics, source comparison |
| Digital Safety Quick Reference | TXT | Key terms, glossary, decision pairs, and recall points | Flashcards, glossaries, quizzes |
| CISA — Recognize and Report Phishing | YouTube URL | Public-awareness spoken explanation, subject to transcript availability | Video-transcript questions, cross-source comparison |
| FTC — How to Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams | Web URL | Authoritative public guidance to corroborate the local learning examples | Source-grounded evidence, cross-checking, verification |

## 🎯 Main Learning Outcomes

- Convert information from different source types into clear explanations and summaries.
- Ask specific questions and trace answers to supporting source passages.
- Generate visual, audio, presentation-based, and assessment-based learning outputs.
- Compare information across different sources and identify where evidence is missing.
- Distinguish fictional teaching scenarios from published public guidance.
- Appreciate AI limitations: fluent responses do not guarantee factual accuracy.

## 🔎 Practical Boundaries

The six selected resources are **inputs** to the learning project. The Gemini Notebook project and its generated outputs (audio/video overviews, slide decks, infographics, reports, quizzes, and others) are **not included in this source pack** and are created separately within a supported Google account.

Support for online links, file processing, captions, account features, and source limits may change. Outputs should be checked against the original sources before they are reused or distributed.
