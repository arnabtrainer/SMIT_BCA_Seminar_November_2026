# 🗂️ Source Map and Learning Objectives

**Project:** Stay Smart in the Digital World  
**Universal module:** AI Can Help Me Learn — Transform Information into Knowledge

| Source | Type | Unique contribution | Best NotebookLM uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital Safety Fundamentals | PDF | Structured concepts, safe procedures and foundation questions, sections F1–F6 | Summary, Q&A, mind map, citations, audio overview |
| Real-World Digital Safety Cases | DOCX | Six fictional, practical decisions (C01–C06), including one ambiguous notification | Scenario MCQs, report, lesson comparisons |
| Digital Safety Awareness | PPTX | Visual communication, diagrams, checklists and cases | Slide synthesis, infographic, source comparison |
| Digital Safety Quick Reference | TXT | Definitions, glossary, decision pairs and recall points | Flashcards, glossary, quizzes |
| CISA Recognize and Report Phishing | YouTube | Public-awareness spoken explanation (transcript if available) | Video-based source questions, comparison |
| FTC How To Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams | Web URL | Authoritative published advice to corroborate local learning examples | Source-grounded evidence, verification |

## Main learning outcomes

- Convert mixed-source information into clear explanations.
- Ask specific questions and trace answers to supporting source passages.
- Produce visual, audio and assessment-based learning materials.
- Separate fictional teaching scenarios from published public guidance.
- Appreciate AI limitations: fluency does not guarantee factual accuracy.

## Practical boundaries

The six inputs are source materials. The Notebook project and its Studio-generated outputs are **not included in this pack** and must be produced and checked in an eligible Google account. Public links and source-import behaviour can change over time.
